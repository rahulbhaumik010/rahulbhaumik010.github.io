Project Overview

This project, part of my doctoral research, explored the design and development of innovative textile-based interfaces for automotive interiors. The goal was to create a 3-dimensional, touch-sensitive cover for the middle console that integrates seamlessly into the car's design and enhances user interaction.

Key Features

  • Explored the affordances of textile surfaces for tactile interaction.
  • Integration of textiles in 3D surfaces for an intuitive multi-surface interaction.
  • Focus on enhancing the user experience within automotive environments.

Status

Completed. The work is part of the doctoral thesis Affordances of Soft Interfaces: A Design and Fabrication Approach (2026), and the in-car 3D-surface study was published at ACM AutomotiveUI 2024.
